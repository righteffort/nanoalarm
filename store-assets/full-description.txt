NanoAlarm is a minimal proof-of-concept alarm clock app that does one thing: it sets a single, one-time alarm and rings it reliably, using system default behaviors and mechanisms.

What NanoAlarm does:
• Sets a single one-time alarm for a specific time
• Rings reliably, exactly on schedule — not late, not silently skipped because the phone was idle or in a power-saving mode
• Wakes the screen and shows a full-screen alert over the lock screen
• Plays sound loud enough to hear even if your phone is set to silent or Do Not Disturb

What it doesn't do yet:
This is an early, intentionally minimal build. It does not currently support repeating alarms, snooze, custom sounds, multiple simultaneous alarms, or recovery after a device reboot — an alarm scheduled before a reboot is lost. These are deliberately out of scope while the core alarm reliability is being proven out.

If you want a full-featured alarm clock app, this isn't it yet.
